# PORTFOLIO PERSONA & DESIGN SYSTEM RULES
# Persona Ratio: 70% MBA Student / Strategy + 30% BCA Systems Enabler

## 1. Core Persona Manifesto (MANDATORY)
> "I do not do any coding. I observe problem, think, get solution, and solve through AI and agent coding."

- **70% MBA Student (Strategy, Product & Business Operations)**:
  - Observes real-world operational friction on the ground (honed over 4+ years leading lending operations at JD Finance).
  - Thinks strategically: analyzes unit economics, credit risks, loan lifecycle bottlenecks, margins, stakeholder workflows, and business processes.
  - Formulates clear business solutions, PRDs, operational workflows, and product roadmaps.

- **30% BCA Student (Systems Enabler & AI Orchestrator)**:
  - Technical literacy: deep comprehension of data schemas, relational models, APIs, state machines, and systems architecture.
  - Does NOT do manual, line-by-line coding or developer boilerplate.
  - Leverages modern AI tools and agentic coding (e.g. Gemini, Antigravity, Claude) to build, test, and ship complete, functional, production-ready software.

---

## 2. Planning & Design Boundaries (STRICT)

1. **NO Developer Machinery or Code Playground Simulators**:
   - Never introduce code-centric simulators, raw source code viewers, developer debugging consoles, or engineer playground components.
   - The user is NOT an entry-level software engineer or coder. Do not frame the portfolio as a software developer portfolio.

2. **Lead with Business Friction & Real Impact**:
   - Every project, case study, metric, and narrative must follow this exact flow:
     1. **Ground Problem Observed**: What operational or financial bottleneck was happening?
     2. **Strategic Thinking**: What business logic, unit economics, or user workflow solves it?
     3. **AI & Agent-Driven Delivery**: How was the production software specified and shipped through AI/agent coding?
     4. **Business Outcome**: What measurable metric, cost reduction, or speed increase was achieved?

3. **Language & Copywriting Guidelines**:
   - Prioritize terms: *Product Operations, Business Operations, FinOps, Workflow Automation, Unit Economics, Credit & Lending Systems, Product Roadmaps, AI-Assisted Delivery*.
   - Avoid developer-only jargon presented without business context (e.g. do not obsess over compiler flags, syntax trivia, or low-level algorithms).
   - Whenever technical competence is mentioned, frame it as: *"Strong systems literacy that allows me to direct AI coding agents with zero hallucination and ship working products."*

4. **Future Feature Proposals**:
   - Any new section or interactive tool proposed MUST serve a business, analytical, or executive purpose (e.g. interactive business metrics, workflow diagrams, PRDs, lending economics breakdowns, or operational case studies).
